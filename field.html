Field entry sign-in

Only individually approved participants can submit samples. Sign in once while connected before going into the field. After that, you can open field entry and save samples offline, then reconnect to submit them.

Email accounts still need individual approval before they can use Field entry.

Checking access...

Record a sample

Choose the site and date, enter the measurements and note the conditions at the site. Leave measurements you did not take blank. You can save multiple samples on this device and submit them when connected.

Preparing offline access...

When offline, the site list comes from the last successful connection. Check the site name before saving.

Submissions are reviewed before appearing in the public data. Please don't enter names or other personal information.

Measurements

Enter at least one measurement. Use a decimal point for decimals. YSI Pro2030 or YSI ProSolo readings are collected in the field; pH and turbidity may be added later from the lab.

Where no unit is shown, check the approved method and instrument. Unusual-value prompts are reminders to check, not limits or safety advice.

Site observations (optional)

Note conditions that may help explain the measurements. Keep each entry brief and do not include names or other personal information.

Saved samples

Samples stay on this device until you submit or delete them. They are not sent automatically.

Submit a site photo

Upload when connected. Choose a photo of the site without identifiable people. Photos are reviewed before they appear publicly; do not include names or other personal information.

Photos are resized and camera metadata is removed before upload. The original remains on your device.
