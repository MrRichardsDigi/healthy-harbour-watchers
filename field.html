Record a sample

Choose the site and date, then enter the measurements you took. Leave other measurements blank. You can save multiple samples on this device and submit them when connected.

Preparing offline access...

When offline, the site list comes from the last successful connection. Check the site name before saving.

Submissions are reviewed before appearing in the public data. Please don't enter names or other personal information.

Measurements

Enter at least one measurement. Use a decimal point for decimals.

Where no unit is shown, check the approved method and instrument. Unusual-value prompts are reminders to check, not limits or safety advice.

Saved samples

Samples stay on this device until you submit or delete them. They are not sent automatically.
